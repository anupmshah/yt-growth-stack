# Product vision

YT Growth Stack helps creators find defensible YouTube opportunities by combining competitor data, wider web evidence, and a voice-first research agent. Its core promise is not unlimited AI ideas; it is traceable reasoning from source evidence to recommendation.

## Product principles
- Voice is the primary control surface; text is always available.
- The conversation shows work, progress, evidence, failures, and approvals.
- Every recommendation explains why it exists and links to provenance.
- Long-running work is modeled as durable jobs, not hidden inside one request.
- Expensive, destructive, or external actions have explicit human gates.
